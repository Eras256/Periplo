## What Periplo is

Periplo is open-source software: an x402 payment facilitator for Stellar, with a discovery catalog, Soroban contracts and this site. The project also runs public demonstration deployments, **on Stellar testnet only**.

## Testnet only

- Everything running on Periplo's deployments uses Stellar testnet. **Periplo does not operate on mainnet yet.**
- PTEST and any other asset shown in the demo are test assets **with no value**.
- Stellar testnet is reset periodically; testnet data and balances can disappear.

## Non-custodial

Periplo never receives, holds or moves anyone's funds. The facilitator only sponsors the network fee of payments the buyer signs themselves, and refuses to start if its key could move user funds. Periplo has no fiat rail, charges no fees and requires no accounts.

## The public deployments

The testnet facilitator, the demo and this site are provided as is, for demonstration and testing. They can go down, restart, change or stop operating at any time without notice. Do not use them for anything that depends on their availability.

## Acceptable use

Do not use the public deployments to try to disrupt the service, overload it, or affect other people using it. If you find a vulnerability, follow the [security](/en/security) page instead of exploiting it.

## Third-party services

Periplo depends on services it does not control: the Stellar network and its public RPC server, the stellar.expert explorer, GitHub and the hosting providers. Each has its own terms.
